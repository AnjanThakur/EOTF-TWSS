Filter settings and export provenance

The user-provided Chords Web 2.7.0a screenshot shows Brain mode and a 50 Hz notch selected on all six channels. The notch setting is not a sampling rate. Manufacturer documentation describes Brain as a 45 Hz low-pass; the effective response in these recordings has not been measured.

The public recording path inspected saves filtered channel values. The inspected version is 2.7.1a, not the screenshot version 2.7.0a. This supports treating these exports as likely already processed, while the exact capture build and scaling remain unverified. No additional filters were applied or undone.

Frequency peaks and attenuation belong to the exported signal, which may include prior-filter effects and residual interference. Low-pass filters attenuate rather than erase all higher-frequency components. The folder named data/raw preserves untouched CSV exports; it does not establish that they are unfiltered ADC data.

  • 0 missing/nonfinite/unparseable numeric cells and 0 duplicate complete rows were found. No rows were deleted.
  • 5 non-wrap counter discontinuities were found across 5 files. All occur at acquired sample indices 210–460 (zero-based). Causes remain unverified.
  • The strongest channel correlation is 0.970 between Channel4 and Channel6 in idle. Correlation is not evidence that a channel is faulty or that its signal is neural.
  • 24 of 36 channel/recording spectra have their largest non-DC peak near 0.20 cycles/sample. This would be about 100 Hz if the provisional 500 Hz rate is correct. The repeated peak warrants review of interference and export processing; its cause is not identified by EDA.

  • There are no within-file task markers or verified acquisition timestamps. The mixed file cannot be scored as a labelled YES/NO test from its name alone.
  • No model was trained and no classification accuracy is claimed. Thousands of neighboring samples are not thousands of independent word trials.

What cleaning changed

Original files were copied byte-for-byte and hashed. Cleaned copies keep all rows in source order, preserve every finite signal value and add source-row, segment and quality-flag columns. No filter, interpolation, rereferencing, normalization or amplitude replacement was applied. Large-amplitude flags use a within-file/channel robust deviation threshold of 8; flags do not label samples as artifacts.

Cleaned files therefore remain annotated exports, not artifact-free EEG or a training-ready word dataset. No individual YES/NO labels were invented for the mixed recording.
